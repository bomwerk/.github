# Contributing

Bug reports, accuracy reports and pull requests are welcome.

- **Found a wrong or missing component, version or advisory?** Open an issue with the accuracy report template. These are as welcome as crashes.
- **Want to change code?** Open an issue first for anything larger than a small fix, so we can agree on the approach before you spend time on it.
- **Contributing to the scanner?** Read [the contributing guide in bomwerk/bomwerk](https://github.com/bomwerk/bomwerk/blob/main/docs/CONTRIBUTING.md). It covers the build, the tests, formatting and the hard rules a change must keep.
- **Suspected vulnerability?** Do not open a public issue. See the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Contributions are accepted under the licence of the repository they go to. For bomwerk Community that is Apache-2.0, and there is no separate CLA.

By taking part you agree to follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
